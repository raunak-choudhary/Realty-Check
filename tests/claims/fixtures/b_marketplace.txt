Neighborhood overview
Zilker is one of Austin's most popular central neighborhoods, bordered by Lady Bird Lake to the north and the Barton Creek Greenbelt to the west. Residents walk to the park, swim at the springs year-round, and bike to downtown in about ten minutes. Streets are lined with live oaks, and the housing mix ranges from small post-war bungalows to newer two-story homes. Food trucks and coffee shops cluster along South Lamar and Barton Springs Road.
Commute: downtown is a short drive or bike ride; the airport is about 20 minutes by car outside rush hour.

Getting around
Car-dependent for most errands, but the park, the springs and several restaurants are within a 15 minute walk. Bus lines on South Lamar run every 15 minutes during the day. Bike lanes connect to the Butler Trail around Lady Bird Lake.
