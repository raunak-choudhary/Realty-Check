Explore Memorial
Memorial is one of Houston's most sought-after areas, known for tall pines, top-rated schools and quiet residential streets. Residents enjoy easy access to Memorial Park, City Centre, the Energy Corridor and the Galleria. The area offers a mix of estate homes, townhomes and boutique apartment communities, many tucked behind mature trees along winding roads. Weekends here mean farmers markets, trail runs and patio dining.

Renting in Houston: a neighborhood-by-neighborhood guide
Houston has no zoning, which means apartments, offices and taquerias can sit side by side. That makes neighborhood character change block by block. Inside the Loop you'll find walkable pockets like Montrose and the Heights, while areas such as Memorial and the Energy Corridor trade walkability for trees, space and quieter streets. Think about your commute first: traffic on the major freeways can double a drive at rush hour. Read more

What does "luxury" really mean in a rental listing?
Almost every listing calls itself luxury. Look past the adjectives and ask concrete questions: what year was the building finished, when were the units last renovated, what appliances are included, how is parking handled, and what are the total monthly fees? A quick tour will tell you more than any headline. Read more

Hurricane season checklist for renters
Know your evacuation zone, keep copies of your lease and insurance policy in a waterproof bag, photograph your belongings, and ask your property manager how the building handles flooding and power outages. Renters insurance usually covers your belongings, not the structure. Read more

How to spot a rental scam
Be wary of listings with prices far below the market, owners who cannot meet in person, and requests to pay by wire transfer or gift card. Never send money for a home you have not seen. Read more

Top questions renters ask
Can I negotiate rent? Sometimes, especially for longer leases or during slower months.
Are utilities included? It depends on the property; always ask for an estimate.
How much is the deposit? Typically one month's rent or less, depending on your credit.
Can I have a pet? Many properties allow pets with a deposit and monthly pet rent.

Moving checklist
Eight weeks out: set a budget, research neighborhoods, and start touring.
Six weeks out: submit applications, gather pay stubs, and request references.
Four weeks out: book movers or reserve a truck, and order packing supplies.
Two weeks out: set up electricity, internet and renters insurance at the new address.
Moving week: confirm your move-in time, pick up keys, and walk through the home with your property manager. Take photos of any existing damage and keep them with your lease.
After you move: update your address with the post office, your bank and your employer, and register to vote at your new address.
